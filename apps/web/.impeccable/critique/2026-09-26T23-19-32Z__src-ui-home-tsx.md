## What's Working
1. **The ledger as a record.** Page stock, specimen labels, mono times, a pin that is always visible, columns fixed at rest, and rows that stack below 560px.
2. **Consequence copy where a mistake costs something.** Leave and delete are separate controls. The confirmation sits in the row, beside the name. Sharing never quietly downgrades to a link without keys.
3. **Guests are equals on the board.** The view-only badge is a mono record, not a pill. Faces are named, and the person you follow is never hidden behind "+N".

## Priority Issues

**[P0] Sharing leaves an editable orphan that reports "Saved".** `shareCurrentBoard` disposes the runtime and deletes the local board (`share.ts:227-228`), then shows the links over a canvas that is still live (`ShareControl.tsx:104`). Share stays enabled and the bar reads "Saved". The sheet can't be dismissed and takes no focus. A note typed afterwards reads "Saved" and is gone on reload. This is rule 7's one unacceptable failure. Fix: confirm before moving; after the move, make the board inert or navigate straight to the shared board; disable Share. `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The sheets and the row's modes trap the keyboard and drop focus.**
- **Sign-in sheet:** ignores Escape and outside presses.
- **Share sheet:** ignores Escape and loses focus to `<body>`.
- **Account sheet:** takes no focus, and "Sign out" is 31 Tabs away.
- **Delete confirmation:** ignores Escape, and Keep drops focus to `<body>`.
- **Password form:** has no cancel; "No password" *clears* the password, and "Set" uses the danger style.

Fix: move focus into each sheet on open and back to the trigger on close; add Escape and outside-press close throughout; add Cancel; show "Remove password" only when a password is set. `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] The front-door account chip signs you out on one press.** This is a regression against `AccountControl.tsx:29-33` and against DESIGN.md's navigation bar ("never signs you out on the press"). The chip is also outlined, against "never an outlined chip". Fix: open the same account sheet from the home chip, borderless. `/impeccable harden`

**[P1] At phone width both surfaces run out of the screen, and zoom is disabled.**
- **Home:** the signed-in page is 480px wide at 390, because the header lockup doesn't wrap. The confirmation and password forms collapse to one word per line.
- **Board:** the bar runs past both edges; the account chip and the AGPL Source link are clipped.
- **Zoom:** `user-scalable=no` stops pinch-zoom everywhere (axe, critical).

Fix: wrap the lockup; wrap the row forms below 560px; add a <480px bar step (the account shows only its face, and Source moves into the account sheet); remove `user-scalable=no`. `/impeccable adapt`

**[P1] The share contract no longer describes sharing.** Every new board is born in a room, so the two-link chooser appears only when moving a legacy local board. The everyday owner path is the "Shared" chip, which silently copies the **edit** link, and the view link lives behind a ledger icon. The risk the contract exists to prevent is now the default. Fix: the owner's room chip opens the two-link sheet; a viewer's copies the view link, named as such; consider putting the password beside the links. `/impeccable shape`, then `/impeccable clarify`

**[P2] Control boundaries under 3:1** (B measured). All of these sit at 1.1–1.8:1:
- the home chip's border;

- the active workspace tab;

Beyond that:

`/impeccable polish`
