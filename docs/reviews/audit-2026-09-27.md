# Audit: `apps/web/src`, 2026-09-27

A cross-cutting `/impeccable audit` taken after the ten per-surface critiques (C3 #1–#10). Evidence came from three isolated agents on the running app, in both worlds:

- **accessibility:** axe on 17 states × 2 worlds, focus probes, 200% zoom, reduced motion, and the 12px floor;
- **performance and theming:** a production build, CDP traces, render counts on `board-mixed-*`, and computed-colour diffs between the worlds;
- **responsive, touch and integrity:** 7 viewports, synthesized CDP touch in Chromium, and the detector.

The P1 claims were then checked against the code. What stayed untested: real devices, WebKit, and a real screen reader.

## Implementation integrity verdict

**Pass.** The system is coherent and specific to this product. Every colour is a token pinned by a test, there is no hard-coded world, and the 68 inline styles are all data-driven. Every detector hit is a declared exception already guarded by a test.

## Findings

### P1

1. **Drag re-renders the whole visible board.**
   - `ObjectView.tsx:91-96`: `dragDx`/`dragDy` are subscribed without `drag.ids.has(id)`.
   - 459 renders per 10 moves with 51 visible objects. On 200 objects that is 12 ms of script per move at ×1 and 40–50 ms at ×4, with frame p95 50 ms; on 1k objects, 90 ms at ×4.
2. **Escape from the in-place editor loses focus to BODY**, by keyboard and by mouse. The next Tab restarts about 31 stops from the canvas.
3. **The record panel runs off narrow and short windows, and does not scroll.**
   - It is a fixed 360px wide (`Inspector.tsx:62`) with no max-height. The clamp in `scene/anchoring.ts:104` gives up when the panel is bigger than the space.
   - At 390px, Delete, swatches and opacity are off-screen, and the panel covers the selection's handles. At 844×390 and at 200% zoom it covers the zoom cluster.
4. **Signed out below 480px, "Sign in" is a 16px invisible button**, and the Source link goes with it (AGPL §13).
   - The rule at `styles.css:3206` assumes an avatar is left behind.
   - A regression from C3 #9: `phone-width.spec` covers only the signed-in bar.
5. **The flyout chevrons are 12×50px** (`Toolbar.tsx:216-245`): axe `target-size` (WCAG 2.5.8), on every board.
6. **No pinch-zoom on the board.**
   - `.of-canvas { touch-action: none }` turns off the browser's pinch, and nothing replaces it. A two-finger spread starts a marquee.
   - `styles.css:4915` says touch is out of scope.

### P2

- **Focus lost to BODY when the record panel unmounts:** Escape from a swatch, or pressing its Delete.
- **Comment composer:** `aria-controls` points at a mention list that doesn't exist, and there is no combobox role or `aria-expanded`.
- **A second finger hijacks a drag in progress.**
- **`pointercancel` commits the gesture** (`Canvas.tsx:141`), rather than reverting it the way Escape does. There is also no `lostpointercapture` or blur handling.
- **The nav bar clips on a shared board between 480 and 800px:** Source, and the theme toggle at 390.
- **Comments panel at 390:** it overlaps the rail and Find, and uses `100vh`.
- **No `(pointer: coarse)` enlargement:**
  - most chrome is 30×30;
  - workspace tabs are 26px tall, below DESIGN.md's own 30px floor.
- **Wheel zoom re-renders every visible object:** each one subscribes to `viewport.zoom`.
- **Panning repaints the six-layer gradient ground on every move:** about 26 ms per frame in software raster; likely less on a GPU.
- **Idle hover runs an unindexed whole-document hit test on every move:** 1 ms at 1k objects, 4.4 ms at 10k.
- **One 999KB (288KB gzip) entry chunk.** Supabase (about 209KB) and Yjs (about 78KB) ship to the front door and to local boards, which never use them. Throttled to interactive: about 6.3s.
- **The browser's text-size preference is ignored:** all type is in px.

### P3

- Hover writes to the store twice per move with no equality check. `EndpointOverlay` renders on every hover.
- The Canvas cascades a re-render into about 13 overlays on every pan move.
- A forced layout on every drag and wheel event.
- Board images have no `decoding="async"`.
- No `main` landmark on the board, and the inspector's H2 comes before the H1.
- The password gate uses `form[role=alertdialog]`.
- A mouse click on the canvas does not focus it.
- A pressed toggle's ring reads like the focus ring.
- Empty header cells in tables.
- In After Hours, a frame's default white fill is a bright slab (a design question, compliant with DESIGN.md).
- At 390 on the front door, titles clip at about 18 characters and times wrap.
- At 844×390 the rail scrolls with nothing saying so.
- Drift in `styles.css`:
  - 28 selectors split across several blocks;
  - 3 dead classes;
  - untokenised font weights;
  - two serif stacks;
  - off-scale front-door lengths.

## Positive findings

- **axe:** no violations on the front door (signed in and out), the account sheet, the gates or the start-failed panel. Nothing below 12px anywhere.
- **Contrast:** passes in both worlds, measured on composited pixels.
- **Keyboard:**
  - the Tab order is logical, and every stop draws a 2px ring;
  - focus returns correctly from search, flyouts, sheets, the context menu and the composer;
  - Shift+F10 and the ContextMenu key open the menu.
- **Reduced motion:** "fewer and gentler" as designed. No layout-property animation, and `will-change` appears only while moving.
- **Culling holds DOM size at about 370 elements from 200 to 10k objects:**
  - the cull pass takes 0.4–4.7 ms;
  - boards load in 18–85 ms;
  - idle costs 2 ms per 2 s.
- **Theming:** no colour literal ignores the world. The cursor re-reads ink on a switch, and all 26 native controls take the dark scheme in After Hours.
- **Touch that works:** tap to arm, place and select; one-finger drag, marquee, hand-tool pan and resize; the flyouts. There is no horizontal page overflow at any of the 7 viewports.
