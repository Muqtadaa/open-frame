## Implementation integrity verdict

**Pass.**

- The newer surfaces are built on the same system as everything else: tokens only, the shared primitives (`.of-surface`, `.of-sheet`, `.of-icon-button`, `.of-tip`), drawn icons, and motion that is covered by reduced motion.
- Per-note hooks (`VoteDots`, `ReactionChips`, the poll) use the registry's mark index, return primitives, and cost one lookup on a note with no marks (rules 9 and 10).
- The drift is small and local. `.of-button` has no pressed state, so four surfaces invented one. Beyond that there is one typed glyph, a borrowed `of-mentions__*` list style, and plurals written out 22 times.
- Of the 8 detector hits, 7 are false positives (strings in tests, the picker's spectrum, the front door's ruled ground). The eighth, a `1.25rem`, is real.

## Findings

### P1

**[P1] Every `useMe()` mount refetches the profile, twice**

- **Location:** `hooks/use-identity.ts:27–55`
  - Each mount calls `accounts.current()` and registers its own `onChange`.
  - Both run a Supabase `profiles` query (`adapters/supabase/identity.ts:31,58,78–83`), and supabase-js replays `INITIAL_SESSION` to every new listener.
  - It is now mounted per note with dots or reactions and per poll (`VoteDots.tsx`, `ReactionChips.tsx`, `MarkedRenderer.tsx`), and culling remounts it on every pan.
- **Category:** Performance.
- **Impact:** A signed-in board with 60 voted notes on screen sends about 150 requests as it opens, and more on every pan. This is a rate-limit risk and slow first marks.
- **Recommendation:** One identity store for the page: a cached promise and one subscription, read through `useSyncExternalStore`. `identityFrom` cached per user.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`.

**[P1] In landscape, the record panel sits over the reaction bar**

- **Location:** 844×390, both worlds.
  - The panel `[382,86→742,304]` covers the bar `[386,102→772,152]`.
  - `elementFromPoint` at the Agree button returns `inspector-summary`.
  - 8 of 9 buttons are covered.
- **Category:** Accessibility, Responsive.
- **WCAG:** 2.4.11 Focus Not Obscured (a focused reaction is fully hidden).
- **Impact:** On a phone held sideways you cannot react to the note you selected.
- **Recommendation:** The bar keeps clear of the panel the way the emoji library does: keep-clear, or dock above the panel. Add a landscape case to `reactions.spec`.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

### P2

**[P2] Undo and redo leave the screen on a phone**

- **Location:** The zoom cluster, `ui/ZoomControl.tsx` / `HistoryButtons.tsx`.
  - At 390, undo sits at x=−13.
  - At 320, undo and redo are fully off-screen, and the bar does not scroll.
- **Category:** Responsive.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.10 Reflow.
- **Impact:** A touch user on a small phone has no undo.
- **Recommendation:** Below a measured width, the cluster gives up the slider before it gives up history (− / + stay).
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] The arrange bar's distribute buttons are cut off**

- **Location:** `canvas/ArrangeBar.tsx`. At 390, `distribute-x`/`-y` sit at x=389/431, past the bar and the viewport. Six of 8 controls show at 390, and 4 of 8 at 320.
- **Category:** Responsive.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.10.
- **Recommendation:** Wrap onto a second row, or fold into a menu, when the bar is wider than the board.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] The listen prompt runs off the right edge**

- **Location:** `ui/SessionMusic.tsx` prompt. At 390, Dismiss spans 375→415. At 320, Dismiss is off-screen and Listen is clipped.
- **Category:** Responsive.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.10, 2.5.8.
- **Recommendation:** Anchor it inside the viewport, docked like the other top notices below 520px.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable adapt`.

**[P2] A finger cannot take a dot back**

- **Location:** `interaction/pointer-controller.ts:131`.
  - Taking a dot back by pointer needs Alt, and the banner tells a phone "Alt-click a dot to take it back".
  - A synthesised long-press cast a second dot.
  - Keyboard users have Backspace and the menu.
- **Category:** Accessibility, Responsive.
- **WCAG:** 2.5.1, 2.1.1 (by analogy: a function with no pointer path on touch).
- **Impact:** A misplaced vote on a phone can only be undone with Undo.
- **Recommendation:** A touch-reachable way to remove: an owner decision, see the questions.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable harden`.

**[P2] Poll control edges fall below 3:1**

- **Location:** `styles/objects.css:133,220`.
  - The options use `--of-control-border`, a chrome token, on the card's paper: 2.26:1 on the default card After Hours, and 2.79–3.00:1 on seven card colours in the Notebook.
  - Close poll's edge is 2.16 / 2.30.
  - No test reads `.of-poll`.
- **Category:** Theming, Accessibility.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.11 Non-text Contrast.
- **Recommendation:** Derive the edges from the card's own ink (`currentcolor` at a measured share). Add a contrast test across every card colour in both worlds.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.

**[P2] `.of-button` has no pressed state, and Mute's is colour alone**

- **Location:** `reactions-voting-cluster.css:81`, `timer.css:144`, `music.css:11,48`.
  - These are four private pressed styles. Three use the all-round accent ring that the 09-27 audit retired for the foot bar, and the "edge, not a ring" test only reads `.of-icon-button--on`.
  - Mute (`SessionMusic.tsx:507`) shows pressed by text colour only: 1.14:1 Notebook, 1.34:1 After Hours.
- **Category:** Implementation integrity, Theming.
- **WCAG:** 1.4.1 Use of Color, 1.4.11.
- **Recommendation:** One primitive `.of-button[aria-pressed='true']`, on the icon button's foot bar, with the four private copies deleted and the test extended.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable polish`.

**[P2] The new surfaces all ship in the entry chunk**

- **Location:** The build. The entry chunk is 652 KB (196 KB gzip), against 516 KB (155 KB gzip) at the last audit: +26%.
  - About 235 KB of source in it is open-on-request surfaces: voting, music, overview, cluster, agent changes, history, poll, the picker.
  - There is no `React.lazy` anywhere.
- **Category:** Performance.
- **Recommendation:** Lazy-load the surfaces that open on request, and add an entry-chunk budget to `tools/bench/budgets.ts` so the next one cannot grow it silently.
- **Suggested command:** `/impeccable optimize`.

### P3

- **The person's own dots re-animate on every mount when accounts are on.** `VoteDots.tsx`: what counts as "seen" is captured while `me` is still null, in a hidden round. e2e runs with accounts off, so cannot see it.
- **A finished timer keeps ticking.** `SessionTimer.tsx`: status stays `running` past zero, so the 250ms tick re-renders the whole Session control until reset. `useTick` and `useClock` are one hook written twice.
- **Always-mounted chrome subscribes to the whole document.** This is `StatusBar.tsx:28–29` (the title, and the selection read for `.size`), `BoardOverview.tsx:67` while closed, and `use-voting.ts:50`, which rescans on every structural change in 3 subscribers.
- **The emoji library mounts all 1,914 buttons at once.** In `ReactionPicker.tsx:207`, use `content-visibility` or windowing. axe also flags `scrollable-region-focusable`: the grid walks by arrow keys, and nothing says so to a screen reader.
- **The version history sheet does not keep Tab inside.** It has no `wrapTab`. Every other sheet does.
- **The overview's expand arrow is a typed `▾`/`▸`** (`BoardOverview.tsx:365`); `DisclosureIcon` exists.
- **The type-ramp test lets `rem` through.** Its `endsWith('em')` exemption (`design-tokens.test.ts:694`) admits `1.25rem` (`reactions-voting-cluster.css:360`).
- **The voting banner covers board content at phone width.** A tap on the covered part of a note lands on the hint and casts nothing. The voting status also wraps a word or two per line at 390 and 844.
- **In landscape, the record panel covers a selected poll's answer bars,** and Close poll sits under the zoom bar.
- **Coarse targets below the product's 40px floor.** All pass the 24px AA minimum:
  - `board-exit` 34×40;
  - `share-board` 35×40;
  - the shape and table flyout chevrons 24×50;
  - the volume slider 24 tall;
  - reaction chips 40×24 at 100%;
  - emoji cells 38;
  - `workspace-new` 26 tall.
- **Small drift:**
  - `of-mentions__*` is reused as a list style by history, agent changes and inbox;
  - plurals are hand-written 22 times;
  - `overview.css:15` uses `32px` against the gutter token;
  - the version list is not paginated, and each row runs its own "time ago" interval.

## Patterns

- **The new surfaces were each tested at desktop size.** Landscape and 320 are where they collide: the record panel against the reaction bar and the poll, and bottom and top chrome against the viewport edge. `phone-width.spec` walks portrait 390 only.
- **Per-component reads of global state.** Identity, the document title and the round scan each re-derive state that the page holds once.
- **A state with no primitive gets invented per surface.** Pressed buttons, list rows.

- **Layout:** no horizontal page overflow at any width down to 320, and no text below 12px.
- **Escape:** it never drops focus to BODY on any surface.
- **Tab:** it stays inside the session sheet, inbox, cluster, overview and emoji library.
- **Keyboard voting:** Shift+F10 → Start dot voting → Enter → focus on the Vote toggle.
- **Touch:**

- **Motion:** only `translate`, `scale`, opacity and colour. All of it is answered under reduced motion, and the guard now covers transitions.
- **Theming:** no colour literals, and every colour token in the new CSS has an After Hours value.
